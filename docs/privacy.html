Screenglide – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

Data we collect

Screenglide does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal data. The app has no accounts, no servers, no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking.

Data stored on your Mac

Recordings, cursor and click positions, and projects are saved only on your Mac, inside the app's own storage. Exported videos are saved where you choose. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Permissions

Screen Recording is used only to capture the display you select. Microphone access is used only if you turn the microphone on. System audio is captured only if you turn it on. Cursor and click positions are recorded only while a recording is running and are used to add zoom and cursor effects.

Children

Screenglide collects no information from anyone, including children.

Contact

Questions? Open an issue at github.com/svashchenko8877/screenglide/issues.
