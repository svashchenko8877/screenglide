Screenglide – Support

Report a problem or ask a question

Open an issue at github.com/svashchenko8877/screenglide/issues and include your Mac model and macOS version. Please do not post private recordings.

Screen recording does not start

Open System Settings > Privacy & Security > Screen & System Audio Recording, switch Screenglide on, then reopen the app.

No microphone or system audio

Turn on the Microphone / System audio switches on the home screen. Microphone access is requested the first time you use it.
